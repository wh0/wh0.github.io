Really all the hard problems have been solved.
Use markdown so that writing isn't too verbose.
Make fancy text effects in SVG.
Don't actually make your own SVG, use a little service that generates it.

All that's left is the question of how to line up the baseline of text inside an SVG image with the baseline of the text outside.

Codex ("Codex is OpenAI's coding agent") seems to have a way to do it.

![](/assets/2026/codex-sub-line.png)

Sort of.
It's not perfect.

![](/assets/2026/codex-sub-line-zoom.png)

What are they doing?

```md
**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/...)</sub></sub>  Reject f... on unsupported backends**
```

They add `<sub>` ([subscript](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/sub)) until it's approximately right.
